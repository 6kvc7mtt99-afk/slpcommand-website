# 07 — Content engine

Quality > volume. Empty week > wrong week.

## Pipeline

```
1 research object (keyword or VOC pain)
  → source pack (BILC + 3 SERPs + product truth)
  → 1 page or article (Claude draft, founder accuracy pass)
  → 5 X / 2 IG / 1 Reel / optional YT chapter
  → 1 FAQ already on the page
  → App Store promo variant (monthly)
```

No email until an ESP exists. No scheduled Reddit.

| Step | Who |
